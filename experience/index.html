Robotics Engineer

At Blue Atlas Robotics, I worked as a Robotics Software Engineer developing and maintaining the software for our underwater ROVs. Most of my day-to-day work was in Python, C/C++, and ROS2, alongside joining client demonstrations as technical support. My main tasks included:

  • Migrating the robot's proprietary codebase over to ROS2.
  • Setting up Nvidia Jetson Xavier boards, integrating GMSL cameras, focusing the lenses and performing the stereo-pair camera calibration.
  • Writing ROS2 interfaces and drivers for onboard sensors.
  • Building computer vision modules in OpenCV for underwater camera feeds.
  • Building an update tool so clients could update their robots' software without needing technical know-how.
